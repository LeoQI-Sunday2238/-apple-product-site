Apple Intelligence

The next generation of Apple Intelligence powers helpful features across the system, simplifying the things users do every day. It integrates the latest Apple Foundation Models deep into Apple's platforms and is uniquely designed to protect users' privacy, with on-device processing and Private Cloud Compute.

  • On-device AI processing protects user privacy
  • Private Cloud Compute for complex AI workloads
  • Deep system-level integration across iPhone, iPad, Mac
  • Personal context understanding across apps
访问 Apple 美国官网
来源：Apple Newsroom WWDC 2026 官方发布

Siri AI

Siri AI is an entirely new version of Siri, powered by Apple Intelligence. A profoundly more capable and conversational assistant with personal context understanding, broad world knowledge, and on-screen awareness. It can help users find what they need across messages, emails, photos, and more, with a new dedicated app to revisit conversations.

  • Understand screen context and personal conversation history
  • Search across messages, photos, emails and calendar
  • New dedicated Siri AI application for conversation history
  • More conversational and natural language interaction
访问 Apple 美国官网
来源：Apple Newsroom WWDC 2026 官方发布

iOS 27

iOS 27 brings all-new Siri AI, AI-powered photo editing with Spatial Reframing and upgraded Clean Up, Siri mode in Camera, intuitive child safety features, and system-wide performance improvements. Coming this autumn.

  • Built-in Siri AI assistant across whole system
  • AI photo editing: Spatial Reframing and Clean-Up
  • Camera Siri mode, new child safety protection tools
  • Optimized system performance and power efficiency

macOS 27 Golden Gate brings all-new Siri AI, next-generation Apple Intelligence across the apps you use most, Visual Intelligence on Mac, and enhanced Liquid Glass design. Available starting September 15.

  • Visual Intelligence on Mac — learn more and take action about virtually anything onscreen
  • Enhanced Liquid Glass with improved readability, contrast and customizable appearance

watchOS 27

watchOS 27 brings Siri AI with a dedicated Siri app, a new dynamic app grid, single tap gesture, Workout Buddy with new fitness insights, and expanded Cycle Tracking support. Available starting September 15.
